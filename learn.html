Practice project · In development

A place to practice. Confidence for your own health portal.

Tracing Care is a practice health portal filled with made-up information. You can click around, make mistakes, and learn at your own pace before you use the portal at your own doctor's office.

Everything here is pretend. Never type your real health details or your portal password into Tracing Care.

Why I'm building it

Learning the portal should feel manageable.

Health portals now hold your visits, messages, medicines, and test results. Many people are expected to learn them alone, with their real information on the screen. Tracing Care gives you a calm place to learn first.

Explore without pressure

Nothing you do here changes a real record or reaches a real doctor. Click anything, get it wrong, and try again.

Build skills that carry over

The practice portal is laid out like the ones many clinics use, so what you learn here helps you find your way in your own portal.

Everyday health portal skills

Small steps toward greater confidence.

Find your way around

Learn where things live: home, messages, test results, visits, and bills.

Prepare for an appointment

Find the date, time, and doctor, and read any notes about getting ready.

Understand messages and results

Read a message from the care team and find a test result, with plain-language help.

Find medicines and refills

See your list of medicines and practice asking for a refill.

Recognize privacy and sharing settings

Learn how family helpers can be given access, and how to spot a scam message.

Know when to ask for help

Know when a portal message is fine, and when to call the clinic instead.

A look inside the practice portal

Meet Robin Merriweather, our made-up patient. You'll help Robin with everyday tasks, like finding a visit or asking for a refill.

The learning approach

See it. Try it. Make it familiar.

  1. 01

    Start with what matters to you

    Pick a task you'll actually use, like checking a visit or asking for a refill.

  2. 02

    Walk through it together

    Guided Practice gives one step at a time, with a "Show me where" button if you get stuck.

  3. 03

    Practice again at your own pace

    Start over anytime. Repeat a task until it feels easy.

  4. 04

    Connect it to your own portal

    Use the same steps in your own doctor's portal, knowing what to look for.

A learning space with a clear purpose

Tracing Care is for practice only.

  • It is not connected to any doctor, clinic, hospital, or health plan.
  • It does not give medical advice or care.
  • Nothing you do here sends a real message, request, or payment.
  • Every name, record, clinic, and pharmacy is made up.
